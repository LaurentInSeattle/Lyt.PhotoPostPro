
# Photo Rebel 

Photo Rebel is a **complete** software solution for the Digital Photography enthousiast, amateur or pro, beginner or experimented.


- Non-Destructive Edits
- Complete 'Raw' Support
- MTP Camera Connexion
- Floating Point Editing and Image Processing
- Extensive Image Library Management
- 'Pro' Exports: file formats, borders, watermarks, signatures

# Summary 

Photo Rebel **Connects** to your camera (or iPhone) and download the images you pick to your Personal Computer.

Photo Rebel **Organizes** and classiflies the images on your PC storage system, analysing EXIF metadata, building a well and clearly structured photo library.

Photo Rebel is also able to **Import** any images from any storage device, like a secondary disk or zip drive you might have, into your library. 

Photo Rebel helps you tp **browse** your library and easily find the images you are looking for.

Photo Rebel assists you with the somewhat 'painful' and time consumming process of **culling and rating** your pictures.

Photo Rebel **edits your photos** providing advanced tools and a solid workflow designed with the help of professional photograpphers.

Photo Rebel **Exports** your edited photos in the best usage size and format, for print, web publishing or gallery.

Photo Rebel can **sign and watermark** your photos: Customize borders, signature text, fonts, style, placement, colours.

Photos have been shot to be seen: The Photo Rebel **gallery** will showcase them: Full screen slide-Show, Set images as Desktop Background.

Photo Rebel **speaks your language**: Localized by fluent and native (human) speakers to: French, Italian, Japanese, Spanish (partially - still in progress), English. 10+ other languages machine translated (5) (7)

# Camera Connection 

Click the camera icon on the navigation bar on the left side to enter 'Camera' mode.

Connect your camera or iPhone via USB to your personal computer. 
Thousands of camera model are supported. All MTP compatible devices will connect to Photo Rebel.
Do not forget to turn your camera on or 'wake' it up, if needed.

The User Interface should then display the name of the connected camera and the 'Begin Transfer' button should light up. 
Launch the transfer and watch your photo being downloaded from your device, showing up as thumbnails in the main view. 
Depending on how many images are stored on your camera, this may take a while. 
Once all images have been downloaded or when you interrupt the transfer, you have the oportunity to bring them all in your library 
or select some to ignore, or just pick a few to include in your library.

Once you are done with this selection process, you can optionally delete some or all images in the camera. 
Important Note: Apple iPhone will NOT let Photo Rebel (or any other program) delete images or other files 
from your device.

If movie files or other files have been doanloaded, you can switch to the corresponding view and 
repeat the same selection and delete process.

# Photo Imports 

Click the '+' icon on the navigation bar on the left side to enter 'Import' mode.
You can either drag and drop a folder (containing images) or a single image in the center area.

- Single Image 

Photo Rebel loads the image, displays it and presents its metadata. 
You can add it to your library or begin editing it, which will automatically add it to your library.
Much more on Editing later...

- Image Folder 

Importing from a folder works similarly as importing from your camera, except that the images in the source 
folder are only copied, analysed, and never deleted. It is up to you to delete these images, should you want to avoid 
having duplicates on your collections.


# Your Library 

Click the 'stack of photos' icon on the navigation bar on the left side to enter 'Library' mode.

Images added to your library, either imported or downloaded from your camera are classified by Year, Month and Day.
There are three dates of importance for Photo Rebel: 
- Date of Capture : When the photo was shot.
- Date of Addition in your Library : When the photo was actually in your library.
- Date of Last Edit : When you edited the photo for the last time.

The library view top area let you select any combination of date kind with any choice of Year, Month and Day.
The library view then filters and shows the corresponding images.
Additionally, you can also filter using 'Stars' and 'Tags'. 
More about these two concepts in the following sections.


# Tagging

Tagging your photos with keywords, locations, and names is easy with Photo Rebel, it is also optional... But strongly recommended.

Each time an image is selected in the library, a larger thumbnail and its metadata is shown in the right panel, 
along with a 'Tags' section. 
Clicking the button with a tag icon will let you edit the tags attached to that particular picture.


# The Star System 

With Photo Rebel the Star System is simple and straightforward: 

- Zero Star: this photo has never been reviewed yet.

- One Star: Poor image, keep for now, but consider delete.
- Two Stars: Not so good.
- Three Stars: Not bad at all.
- Four Stars: A keeper.
- Five Stars: Gallery worthy photo, your best!


This rating system is very similar to what is available in almost all other photo editors. 
Photo Rebel adds the following tweaks: 

- Zero Star: This default rating is used when launching a culling process.

- Five Stars: Those images are aautomatically added to your 'Gallery'. (See corresponding section) 

Each time an image is selected in the library, a larger thumbnail and its metadata is shown in the right panel, 
along with the 'Tags' section. Clicking the stars will let you change the rating given to that particular picture.

# Culling 

Click the 'stack of photos' icon on the navigation bar on the left side to enter 'Library' mode.
Choose the 'Added' filter at the top left, navigate the date buttons to find the photos you want to rate and cull.
Photos added on that day will show up as thumbnails. All photos there are all with zero stars, unrated. 

Clicking "Rate and Cull" will launch the culling workflow.

Then Photo Rebel will ask you to define the tags that will be attached to all the photos you will 
decide to keep. For example, if you are sorting the pictures of your recent road trip, you will possibly enter "Travel", "Summer", "Cafifornia", "Yosemite".
No need to enter specific dates, as Photo Rebel is already doing that. No need to enter an exact location if your camera 
records GPS locations.

While you are thinking about the tags and typing them, Photo Rebel loads in the background medium size versions 
of the photos in the current set.

Then you can select images in the "film strip" on the left side. Multiple selection is possible and three cases are possible: 

- Three or more images selected: Larger images are presented in a grid fashion, consider which ones to keep and / or which
ones to reject.

- Two images : Choose the one to keep, it will be given automatically one star and the other one will rejected 
and removed from your library. The image you choose to keep is then shown in single image view.

Single image: 

In this view, you can add or remove stars, or even reject that photo, edit tags for this particular image. 
You can also modify the set of tags automatically added during the culling process.
